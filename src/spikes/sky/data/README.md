- **HYG Database v4.4**, David Nash / Astronexus and its catalog contributors: <https://codeberg.org/astronexus/hyg>. See `HYG-LICENSE.txt`, upstream acknowledgments and the fixed source URLs in `manifest.json`.
- **Chinese Song Dynasty Sky**, Sun Shuwei (孙殳玮), contributed to Stellarium: <https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/master/skycultures/chinese_song_dynasty>. Original description, references, author credit and license are retained in `Song-description.md`.
- **Modern Sky**, Stellarium's team, official v26.3 source: <https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/v26.3/skycultures/modern>. Original description/author/license notice retained in `Modern-description.md`. Chinese names come from the official `zh_CN.po` skyculture translations; no names were guessed. All 88 entries are present, including the 85 source image mappings. Upstream has no separate image for Puppis, Serpens or Vela. PNGs are unchanged; source URL, SHA-256 and byte count of each are in `manifest.json`. Runtime shows up to eight visible drawings (five on narrow screens), prioritizes the selection, and uses a bounded texture cache.

Adaptations by the LuBirth sky spike, 2026-10-21: select HYG stars brighter than or equal to V=6.5 plus all HIP references used by this culture, exclude the Sun, retain only the fields listed in `catalog.json.columns`, mark unavailable Cartesian states as null, and extract culture IDs, names and polylines. No star positions or historical names were invented. Fixed versions, source hashes, output hash and completeness counts are in `manifest.json`. Regenerate with `python3 scripts/prepare-sky-spike.py`; this accesses only the pinned public source URLs and caches source bytes in the system temporary directory.

2026-10-22 extension: retain all modern line/image anchors as well (four additional faint stars, 8,945 total). `--stellarium-assets-root /path/to/official/v26.3/skycultures/modern` can reuse an official desktop release's PNGs after checking that its index matches the pinned source. Modern images retain the source pixel/HIP anchors and are projected onto the same sky sphere, not placed as arbitrary screen overlays. Chinese 28 is a filtered set of 28 primary asterisms from the Song data, not a claim about verified mansion-sector boundaries.

`hipAliases` explicitly records two **component proxy anchors**: HIP 55203 → HYG 118743 (HD 98230), HIP 78727 → HYG 119059 (HD 144069). These HIP system entries lack precise astrometry in the primary Hipparcos catalog. The identifiers are cross-checked against the ESA 1997 catalog [I/239/hip_main, hosted by CDS VizieR](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/I/239); the two-row identifier query is retained in `HIP-component-identifiers.tsv`, including its source and rights URL. The HYG component's coordinates and identity are retained unchanged. A component is **not** a verified system photocentre; orbital modeling and historical validity remain unverified. No synthetic star was added to fill these gaps.

`lunarSystemUnverified` preserves upstream data and its **“Needs confirmation”** comment. The spike does not calculate or claim a verified lunar mansion assignment. M7 and M44 are explicitly omitted rather than coerced into HIP IDs. Rendering-entry counts must not be presented as a count of historical Xingguans. The star catalog is J2000; the historical culture describes a reconstruction, not raw Song-era astrometry.

## 2026-10-22 presentation assets

- `milky-way-density.png` is derived from [NASA SVS / Ernie Wright, Deep Star Maps 2020](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/). The selected **equatorial** `milkyway_2020_4k.exr` already omits bright Hipparcos/Tycho stars; it is not the `_gal` coordinate version. `scripts/prepare-sky-background.mjs` performs trimmed downsampling, wrapped low-pass filtering and display exposure. Source/output hashes and coordinate mapping are in `milky-way-density.json`. It is a fixed-J2000 density background, not a star-free photograph or calibrated photometry. NASA source credits/terms remain separate from the catalog license above.
- `chinese-art/` now contains two **photo-referenced Yuhuang Temple adaptations**, 心月狐 and 尾火虎 (`*-yuhuang-v2.png`). Photographs by **Patrick20242023**, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), from the [Commons category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Statues_of_the_Twenty-Eight_Mansions_in_Jade_Emperor_Temple,_Fucheng). The AI-assisted contemporary adaptations by LuBirth are distributed under **the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license**. Changes include conversion to a transparent colored drawing, removal of the setting, simplified ornaments and interpreted obscured portions. These are not conservation reconstructions, museum-endorsed assets or Stellarium Free Art License illustrations.
- `chinese-art/manifest.json` records each original photo's URL, author, license and SHA-1, each drawing's SHA-256, pixel size and three HIP controls, and what is observed versus interpreted. The [reference folder](../../../../reference/sky/yuhuang-temple/README.md) retains the 28-file API metadata, attributed photo contact sheet, generation prompts and rejected first versions. The old scholar / riding-tiger designs are no longer runtime assets.
- The two Yuhuang figures now have separate **night-sky contour** (`*-yuhuang-line-v1.png`) and **full-color portrait** (`*-yuhuang-v2.png`) assets. Manifest schema 3 keeps `image` for the unmodified full-color portrait and `skyImage` for the sky. Both pairs are 1086×1448 with matching registration. Night contours share one ivory tint and 0.70 opacity, including when selected; original color is shown in the detail panel. The line conversion reduces ornament detail and retains transparent interiors, under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license. Prompts are in `reference/sky/yuhuang-temple/line-art-generation.json`.
- The current registration recipe preserves portrait aspect ratio, using J2000 tangent-plane widths **13° / 16°**. Nearby HIP controls are **not** added to historical asterism membership. The celestial placement is an authored composition, not a historical iconographic map. Tests check geometric mapping and equal angular scale per image pixel, not museum attribution. The full 28-image set remains required; two drawings are not final completion.
- Browser decoding caps large GPU textures at 896 px (640 px on narrow screens), preserving original source PNGs and normalized registration. The cache budgets decoded RGBA + mipmaps, retains at most 12 entries / 64 MiB and disposes unused textures on eviction.
